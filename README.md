# Certified Snacker

A calm weekly meal planner for one household: a laptop week grid you drag recipes onto, a phone shopping list in Waitrose aisle order, honest nutrition, a two-axis rating (stars × how often), gym-day and cycle-aware nudges, and a remembered-price shop estimate.

- Start here: `CLAUDE.md`, then `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` and `docs/RUNBOOK.md`.
- What it will and won't do: `docs/LIMITATIONS.md`.
- Why things are the way they are: `docs/DECISIONS.md`.
